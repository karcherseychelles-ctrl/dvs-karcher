📋 Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)
SOPSteps
Machine intake1. Greet customer & inspect machine physically  2. Complete delivery note & job card  3. Note all accessories received  4. Assign job reference  5. Give customer their copy of delivery note
Diagnosis1. Assign to technician & update status to "In Diagnosis"  2. Document root cause in findings  3. List all parts required  4. Prepare quotation  5. Contact customer for approval before proceeding
Parts ordering1. Add parts to order tracker  2. Contact authorised Kärcher supplier  3. Record supplier info, ETA & cost  4. Update status to "Awaiting import" or "Ordered"  5. Notify customer of any delay
Repair1. Update status to "In Repair" after approval  2. Issue spares to technician — record in tracker  3. Technician completes repair  4. Update to "QC Check"  5. QC inspection completed by senior staff
QC & handover1. Machine tested under load  2. QC passed — update to "Ready for Collection"  3. Contact customer  4. Customer collects — verify signature on job card  5. Invoice issued & payment collected  6. Mark as "Completed"
Warranty claim1. Verify warranty validity with serial number  2. Create WR job card  3. Document fault with photos  4. Submit claim to Kärcher  5. Repair per Kärcher warranty terms  6. File claim documentation
